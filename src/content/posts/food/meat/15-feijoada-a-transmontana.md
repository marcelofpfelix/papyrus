Feijoada a Transmontana is a rich bean stew from the north, usually served with
rice.

## Ingredients

- Red or white beans
- Pork ribs, ear, or belly
- Chourico and morcela
- Cabbage
- Onion, garlic, paprika, and bay leaf

## Method

Cook the beans and meats until soft, add cabbage near the end, and serve with
plain rice.
