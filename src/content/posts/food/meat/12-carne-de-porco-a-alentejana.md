Carne de Porco a Alentejana pairs paprika-marinated pork with clams and crisp
potatoes.

## Ingredients

- Pork shoulder, cut into cubes
- Garlic, paprika, bay leaf, and white wine
- Clams
- Potatoes for frying
- Coriander and pickled vegetables

## Method

Marinate the pork, brown it, then steam the clams in the pan juices. Fold in the
fried potatoes and finish with coriander and pickles.
