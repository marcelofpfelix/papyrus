Leitao a Bairrada is famous for crisp skin, peppery seasoning, and juicy meat.

## Ingredients

- Suckling pig
- Garlic, coarse salt, pepper, and lard
- Bay leaf
- Orange slices for serving
- Simple salad or chips

## Method

Rub the pig with seasoning, roast until the skin is crisp, and serve in slices
with a bright, simple garnish.
