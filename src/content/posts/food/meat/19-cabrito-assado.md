Cabrito Assado is a celebratory roast, often served with potatoes and greens.

## Ingredients

- Kid goat pieces
- Garlic, bay leaf, paprika, and white wine
- Olive oil
- Potatoes
- Parsley and lemon

## Method

Marinate overnight, roast slowly with potatoes, and baste until the meat is
tender and the potatoes are browned.
