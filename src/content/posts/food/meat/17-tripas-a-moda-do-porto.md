Tripas a Moda do Porto is a hearty stew of tripe, beans, sausage, and aromatic
spices.

## Ingredients

- Cleaned tripe
- White beans
- Chourico and smoked meats
- Onion, carrot, garlic, and bay leaf
- Cumin and pepper

## Method

Cook the tripe until tender, then stew it with beans and smoked meats until the
sauce is thick and savory.
