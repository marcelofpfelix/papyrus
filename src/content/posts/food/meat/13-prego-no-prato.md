Prego no Prato is the plated version of the Portuguese garlic steak sandwich.

## Ingredients

- Thin beef steak
- Garlic, butter, and bay leaf
- Fried egg
- Rice, fries, and salad
- Mustard

## Method

Sear the steak in garlic butter, fry the egg, and serve with the classic sides
and a spoonful of sauce from the pan.
