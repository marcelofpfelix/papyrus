Cozido a Portuguesa is a generous one-pot meal built from meats, smoked
sausages, cabbage, potatoes, carrots, and turnips.

## Ingredients

- Beef shank or brisket
- Pork ribs or belly
- Chourico and farinheira
- Cabbage, potatoes, carrots, and turnips
- Salt, pepper, and bay leaf

## Method

Simmer the meats first, then add the vegetables in stages so everything finishes
tender. Serve the broth, meats, and vegetables together on a wide platter.
