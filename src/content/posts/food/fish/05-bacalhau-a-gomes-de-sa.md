Bacalhau a Gomes de Sa is a Porto cod classic with potatoes and plenty of olive
oil.

## Ingredients

- Desalted cod
- Potatoes
- Onion and garlic
- Hard-boiled eggs
- Olives, parsley, and olive oil

## Method

Bake cod, sliced potatoes, and onions together, then finish with eggs, olives,
parsley, and more olive oil.
