Bacalhau a Bras is one of the most familiar Portuguese cod dishes.

## Ingredients

- Desalted shredded cod
- Onion and garlic
- Matchstick potatoes
- Eggs
- Black olives and parsley

## Method

Soften the onion, add cod and potatoes, then fold in beaten eggs off direct heat
so the mixture stays creamy.
