Caldeirada de Peixe is a layered fisherman's stew built without much stirring.

## Ingredients

- Mixed firm fish
- Potatoes
- Tomato, pepper, onion, and garlic
- White wine
- Parsley, coriander, and olive oil

## Method

Layer vegetables and fish in a pot, season well, cover, and simmer gently until
the potatoes and fish are cooked.
