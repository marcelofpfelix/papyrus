Polvo a Lagareiro is tender octopus with roasted potatoes and generous olive
oil.

## Ingredients

- Octopus
- Small potatoes
- Garlic
- Olive oil
- Greens for serving

## Method

Simmer the octopus until tender, roast it with crushed potatoes and garlic, then
finish with plenty of hot olive oil.
