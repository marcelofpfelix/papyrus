Cataplana de Peixe uses a sealed pan to steam fish with vegetables and wine.

## Ingredients

- Firm white fish
- Clams or prawns
- Tomato, pepper, onion, and garlic
- White wine
- Coriander and olive oil

## Method

Layer everything in the cataplana, close it, and cook until the fish is just done
and the broth is aromatic.
