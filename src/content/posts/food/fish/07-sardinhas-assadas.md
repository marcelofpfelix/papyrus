Sardinhas Assadas are simple grilled sardines, especially popular in summer.

## Ingredients

- Fresh sardines
- Coarse salt
- Roasted peppers
- Boiled potatoes
- Bread and olive oil

## Method

Salt the sardines, grill over high heat, and serve immediately with potatoes,
peppers, bread, and olive oil.
