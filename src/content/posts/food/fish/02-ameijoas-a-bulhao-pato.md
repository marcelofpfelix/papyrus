Ameijoas a Bulhao Pato is a fast clam dish with a bright garlic-coriander sauce.

## Ingredients

- Fresh clams
- Garlic
- Olive oil
- White wine
- Coriander and lemon

## Method

Cook garlic in olive oil, add clams and wine, cover until opened, then finish
with coriander and lemon.
