Choco Frito is crisp fried cuttlefish, strongly associated with Setubal.

## Ingredients

- Cuttlefish strips
- Garlic, bay leaf, and lemon
- Flour or cornmeal
- Oil for frying
- Fries and salad

## Method

Season the cuttlefish, coat lightly, fry until crisp, and serve with lemon and
simple sides.
